FAQ

Is Traina medical advice?

No. Traina is general training and nutrition coaching. See a licensed professional for medical matters.

Yazan vs human coach?

Yazan is AI daily guidance. A human coach is a qualified person who reviews your weekly file when enabled.

Account deletion?

In the Traina app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion is permanent after confirmation.
