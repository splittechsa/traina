FAQ / 03

Good questions. Clear answers.

Is the app available now?

Traina is in internal testing. Register your interest for availability updates, without a promised date or paid subscription.

Do I need a gym membership?

The exercise library includes home and gym options. Choose your environment and equipment when preparing your plan.

Is Yazan a human coach?

Yazan is an AI assistant. Human review is a separate service with a named coach, subject to your consent and availability.

How do I control my data?

Withdraw coach sharing consent and request account export or closure in the app. Closure stops access; it does not immediately erase every stored record. Production privacy terms await legal review.
