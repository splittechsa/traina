How it works

A training and nutrition plan from your profile, daily logging, Yazan proposals within safety rules, and human review when subscribed and consented — without inventing outcomes or medical approvals.

Yazan — daily AI coach

Yazan reads what you actually logged: training, food, water, and recovery. He explains your plan and proposes changes inside safety rules — without inventing results or human approvals.

Knows your context

Goals, active program, and the current version — including only approved adaptations.

Proposes, never bypasses

Program changes need your confirmation. Human-review items stay pending until a coach decides.

Separates fact from opinion

Logged ≠ estimated ≠ suggested. Missing data stays unknown.

Human review

On Traina Coach (with your consent), a human coach reviews your weekly file and sends notes that the app clearly labels as human — not Yazan.

FAQ

Is Traina medical advice?

No. Traina is general training and nutrition coaching. See a licensed professional for medical matters.

Yazan vs human coach?

Yazan is AI daily guidance. A human coach is a qualified person who reviews your weekly file when enabled.

Account deletion?

In the Traina app: Settings → Account → Close account. Access ends after confirmation; stored records are not immediately erased.

Contact

Operational questions: support@traina.app. Do not send sensitive health data over insecure email.
