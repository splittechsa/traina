# Traina — Product Vision

**Promise:** *"Your coach is with you every day. Your progress is reviewed by a real person."*

Traina is a Saudi-first, Arabic-first adaptive personal coaching platform. It is **not** a calorie counter, workout library, or generic AI chatbot.

## Commercial opportunity

Traditional personal coaching costs thousands of SAR over a few months. Cheap fitness apps offer tracking without accountability, personalized adjustments, or human review. Traina sells **ongoing coaching** via subscription — AI daily presence plus real human oversight — at a fraction of traditional personal-trainer cost.

## Product principles

1. **Coaching before tracking** — daily logs are infrastructure for coaching outcomes.
2. **Scientifically grounded** — LLM explains; deterministic engines decide what is allowed.
3. **Adaptive, not chaotic** — distinguish daily adherence, recovery, weekly adaptation, and phase changes. Never punish eating with exercise/fasting.
4. **Human oversight is real** — not auto-stamping “approved.”
5. **Privacy by design** — purposes, retention, access, and transfers are explicit.
6. **Saudi-first** — Arabic RTL, natural Saudi tone, premium native mobile UX.

## Packaging (concepts — not final prices)

| Plan | Value |

## Success definition

A member can say: *Traina knows my plan, follows my progress, helps me stay consistent, explains changes, and a real coach reviews my development* — safely, privately, and sustainably.
