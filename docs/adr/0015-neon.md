# ADR 0015 — Neon is the managed PostgreSQL choice

## Status

Accepted. The existing Neon project is linked. Credentials stay in the gitignored API `.env` and are not stored in this repository.

## Decision

Use Neon for managed PostgreSQL. Keep the Fastify API, scrypt sessions, and domain packages. Do not move authentication to Supabase Auth or Neon Auth.

PGlite remains the isolated test and offline-laptop database. It is not the shared production database.

## Why Neon rather than Supabase

Official Neon plan page checked on 2026-10-11:

- Free is $0, with no credit card. Each project includes 100 CU-hours per month, 1 GB of Postgres storage, and 5 GB of egress. Account storage across free projects is capped at 20 GB. Compute suspends after 5 idle minutes and resumes on the next connection. History is 6 hours, capped at 1 GB-month, plus one manual snapshot.
- Launch has no monthly minimum. Published rates include $0.106 per CU-hour and $0.35 per GB-month of storage. History can extend to 7 days. Spending notifications exist on paid plans.
- The connection string is ordinary Postgres with `sslmode=require`. Leaving Neon is a `pg_dump` and restore.

Supabase, from Neon’s August 2026 comparison of the vendor free plans and Supabase’s published Pro price:

- Free is also $0: about 500 MB database, two active projects, and a pause after about a week of inactivity that is restored from the dashboard. No point-in-time recovery on the free plan.
- Pro starts at $25 per month before extra usage.
- Supabase Auth, Storage, and Realtime are useful only if the product adopts them. Sahhti already has session auth. Adopting Supabase Auth would duplicate it and put a privileged key one mistake away from the mobile app.

## What this choice does not claim

- Neither vendor has been verified to host data in Saudi Arabia. PDPL residency is not met by selecting Neon.
- Free-tier compute is exhausted if a connection pool stays open all month. The API closes idle connections after 10 seconds so a quiet database can suspend.
- One million users are not a capacity result.
- Images do not belong in Postgres. Neon object storage, or another S3-compatible bucket, is the media path when media exists. It is not wired yet.

## Cost sketch from published unit prices

These are planning figures, not an invoice.

| Active users | Expected bill while the free allowance holds |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | $0 with scale-to-zero |
| 100 | $0 if monthly compute stays under 100 CU-hours and storage under 1 GB |
| 1,000 | $0 to a few dollars. An always-on 0.25 CU compute is about 180 CU-hours, which is past the free 100 |
| 10,000 | Leave the free plan. Rough Launch spend is on the order of $20–$80 per month if compute is not left at a large size |
| 100,000 | Not estimated as a supported load. Storage, egress, and compute need a measured test before a number is quoted |

That region is not in Saudi Arabia. The phone never receives `DATABASE_URL`. A spending limit was not changed from this session.
