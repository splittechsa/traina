# Store submission checklist

No public store submission is authorized by this engineering pass.

- Resolve P0-PRIV and scientific safety gates, current Apple/Google product/billing policy strategy and territory-specific legal review.
- Verify real store products, server-side verification, signed lifecycle notifications, renew/cancel/restore/refund behavior and entitlement reconciliation in sandbox.
- Produce owned/approved icons, screenshots from actual builds, honest product descriptions, privacy disclosures and accurate account closure/erasure instructions.
- Configure staging/production HTTPS endpoints, Expo project/signing, version identifiers, push credentials and native permission purpose strings.
- Install and verify Arabic RTL and English LTR builds on physical iPhone and Android. Complete workout/offline/nutrition/Yazan/coach/notification/account journeys and accessibility checks.
- Supply actual support contact ownership, review accounts, operational monitoring and release/rollback ownership.
- Attach build IDs, device evidence, reviewers and final GO decision before requesting public submission authorization.
